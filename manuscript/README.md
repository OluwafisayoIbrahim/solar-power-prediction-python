LaTeX bundle supplied for the manuscript. It contains editable LaTeX, the old
PDF, seven source tables, main and supplementary figure PDFs, and Vega-Lite
specifications. The title and six updated reference attributions must be
preserved. This bundle uses superseded 189-row results and is not an updated
scientific output.

The updated LaTeX/PDF must follow the completed experiments under
`results/research_20_days`, retaining the supplied editorial improvements
and adding the recalculated no-fitting voltage/current product to the abstract.
No collection month/year can be inferred from filenames. Small differences
remain descriptive without a significance analysis. All affected findings,
fold counts, diagrams and charts must be regenerated before publication.

The app intentionally withholds the manuscript until
`publication_ready.json` records completion of the content and PDF review.

authorize publication, merge or redeployment.
