# Excluded accidental duplicate

`solar_data_tue_28st.csv` is retained here only for provenance. The researcher
confirmed it was an accidental second save of `solar_data_tue_21st.csv`, not
another measurement day. It must never enter the active research dataset.
The distinct `solar_data_tue_28th.csv` remains active.

All pre-correction experiments with 189 observations and 21 filename groups
are superseded because they included this repeated file. Their predictions,
scores, learning curves and inference exports are preserved as historical
artifacts; they are not corrected research evidence.
